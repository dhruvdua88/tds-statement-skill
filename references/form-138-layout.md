# Form 138 (salary) record layout

Transcribed from the department's own file-format workbook, `Form138_24Q_Q1-Q3_v1.2_22072026.xlsx`, sheet `File Format_R`, published 22-Jul-2026. The published file is named version 1.2 while the sheet's own title still says version 1.1, the same mismatch the Form 140 workbook carries.

`references/form-140-layout.md` is regenerated by `scripts/extract_annexures.py`. This file is maintained by hand, because the extractor is written around the 140 workbook, so re-check the tables whenever the department republishes the Form 138 workbook. The workbook is the authority; where this file and the workbook disagree, the workbook wins.

The statements in this pipeline write these records exactly as described here. Where a remark imposes a rule, the rule is quoted in full.

| Record | Fields | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| FH | 18 | File header, once per file |
| BH | 72 | Batch header, once per batch |
| CD | 30 | Challan detail, once per challan |
| DD | 45 | Employee detail, once per employee |

A regular statement carries exactly one batch, so a file is: one FH, one BH, then for each challan a CD immediately followed by its DD records. Every record carries its full field count, so a line has one fewer `^` than that count.

## How this form differs from Form 140

Both forms were republished on 22-Jul-2026 in the same delimited format, and the FH, BH and CD records share their field numbering and sizes with Form 140. The differences sit in one constant and in the DD record, and they are the ones the builder has to guard.

| Field | Form 140 | Form 138 |
|---|---|---|
| FH 3, File Type | `NS1` | `SL1`, and the utility refuses an SL1 file whose batch header does not say 138 (T-FV-2100: "For File Type SL1, Form Number should be 138") |
| BH 48, 49, 50 | the unmatched challan count and the two salary-detail placeholders, all blank | the same three fields, all blank, but for a different reason: the salary-detail count and gross total belong to the Q4 Annexure II, and T-FV-2135 ("Count of salary statement records must not be provided") keeps the count out of Q1 to Q3 |
| DD 9 | Name of the Deductee | Name of the Employee; 75 characters either way |
| DD 15, Section Code | column E | column F |
| DD 19, 20 | date of payment, column F; amount paid, column G | date of payment, column G; amount paid, column I |
| DD 22, 23 | the two cash-withdrawal amounts under section 393(3) | Remarks 9 and Remarks 10, declared not applicable: T-FV-6434 and T-FV-6436 reject a value in either field on this form |
| DD 24, 25 | Total Tax Deducted and Total Tax Deposited, each with its own conditions | the same two names, but the remarks require the two fields to equal each other, so both carry tax, surcharge and cess as one figure (see below) |
| DD 27, Date of deduction | column N | column H |
| DD 28 | Rate at which Tax Deducted, mandatory, column M | Remarks 11, not applicable. Nothing is written there: T-FV-4093 ("For Form no 140, 144 and 143 Rate at which Tax Deducted/Collected is mandatory") scopes the rate rule away from this form |
| DD 29 | Remarks 9, not applicable | Date of deposit, required to equal the date of the challan the row is paid under (see below) |
| DD 31 | Unique Identification Number of Form 121, column Q | Remarks 13, not applicable |
| DD 32, 33 | reason and certificate number, columns O and P, any Annexure 6 flag | the same two fields, columns N and O, and only flags A and C are valid (T-FV-6444: "Invalid Remark. Only A and C remarks are applicable for selected section code for Form 138.") |

The rest of the two layouts matches field for field. A few names differ in wording only: BH 13 reads TAN of Employer, the responsible-person fields speak of the person responsible for paying salary, and CD 21 and 22 say "Employee/Deductee annexure" where Form 140 says Collectee and Deductor. Section codes come from Annexure 2 of either workbook; `references/section-codes.md` holds the list.

## Fields 4 and 5, the record serial and the challan reference

Field 4 is the record's own serial number, printed as column A of the form, and field 5 is the challan the row belongs to, printed as column B. That is the order the workbook prints and the order Form 140 uses. The legacy 24Q layout printed them the other way round: its field 4 was the challan's serial and its field 5 the deductee's own serial, so anyone carrying a 24Q field map across to this form must not carry that swap with it. The Form 138 workbook's own columns A and B settle the question for this form.

Field 4 runs 1..n across the whole statement rather than restarting at each challan, and must strictly increase: T-FV-4125 is "Deductee detail Record number must be greater than the Deductee detail Record number of previous Deductee record". Field 5 is the challan's own serial number, the value CD field 4 carries, not the bank challan number.

## The two tax figures on a deductee row are one figure

DD field 24 says its value "should be equal to Total Tax Deposited in field no. 25", and field 25 says its value "should be equal to Total Tax deducted mentioned in field no. 24". The builder therefore writes tax, surcharge and cess as one merged figure into both fields. Legacy 24Q spread the same money over three fields, so anyone comparing an old 24Q schedule line by line will find one number where three used to be.

The merge reaches the challan as well. CD field 21 is the sum of the deductee deposit column and CD field 22 the sum of the deductee deducted column; with the two columns equal, both challan fields come out as the sum of the rows' merged tax, which is also what `lint.txt_issues` checks in the written file.

## DD field 29 is a date, whatever its type says

The workbook types DD field 29 as DECIMAL, size 7. That is a workbook defect. The remark on the same row calls it "Date of deposit (dd/mm/yyyy)" and says the value must be in DDMMYYYY format, and the utility requires it to equal the challan's date: T-FV-6489 is "Date of deposit should be same as value in field 'Date of Bank Challan No / Transfer Voucher Number' in Challan Detail Record (CD)." The builder writes field 29 as an eight-character date, the same date as CD field 19 for the challan the row is mapped to.

The department's accepted sample, `138RQ1_sample.txt`, writes an eight-character date here too, which confirms the shape, though not the value: its field 29 is 09052025 while its own challan, CD field 19, is 30042025. Its assessment year (202627) and tax year (202526) are stale as well, since the workbook requires 202728 and 202627 for this tax year. Treat the sample as a shape reference and the FVU messages as the rule.

## Quarter 4 is not implemented

A Q4 statement must also carry the salary-detail records of Annexure II and their child records, and the department has not published the Form 138 Q4 workbook. The workbook in hand is titled for Q1 to Q3 on its face, and the only Q4 table in the research tree, `layout_24q_q4.json`, is the legacy pre-2025-Act 24Q layout, which cannot be mapped onto the new record set without guessing.

Writing a Q4 file without those records produces something the utility rejects: T-FV-2133 is "For Form 138 - Quarter Q4, SD records are mandatory, so count of salary statement records must be greater than 0 (zero)", and T-FV-2142 stops SD records appearing in Q1 to Q3. `layout138.Context` therefore raises a `build_txt.BuildError` naming the quarter, Annexure II and T-FV-2133, rather than write a file that cannot be validated.

## Fields that stay blank

Fields the workbook marks NA, and the optional fields whose remark reads "No value should be specified", are left empty. The three salary-detail placeholders in the batch header, BH 48, 49 and 50, are blank on this form. Every record still carries its full field count, and `build_txt` checks each written line against the counts in `layout138.WIDTHS`, so a layout change that adds a field cannot silently shorten a record.
